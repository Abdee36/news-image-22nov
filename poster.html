COP31 puts its final deal to the vote

After two days in overtime, the summit’s presidents tabled a ten-page package in the early hours of Sunday and called on nearly 200 countries to approve it at a session starting 06:00 GMT. The text never once names fossil fuels, oil, coal or a phase-out.

INDONESIA

3 dead, 36 missing after candy factory fire

Rescuers searched on Sunday for 36 workers after a blaze at the Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in Serang, west of Jakarta. The fire broke out on Saturday; 15 people were rescued alive.

SOUTH KOREA

Minister quits over AI exam-cheating row

Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigned on Sunday and President Lee Jae Myung accepted it, after last-minute anti-cheating rules for the national university entrance exam caused confusion. A top scorer had confessed to cheating with a hidden camera and a phone.

THE GULF

Tankers struck as Iran hits Bahrain site

Iran struck an industrial site in Bahrain and tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, as its Revolutionary Guard claimed US bases were the target. Swimmer Lewis Pugh crossed the strait as a tanker burned nearby.

UNITED STATES

FBI probes hack of its crime database

The FBI is investigating an apparent hack of its National Crime Information Center database, the system federal and local police use to share criminal records. The inquiry was reported on Sunday.
